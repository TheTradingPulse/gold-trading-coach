# Phase 3H — GC Execution Geometry

Tests 36 point-in-time GC configurations across five years of one-minute data: three limit-entry depths combined with original stops, 2/5/10-tick buffers, 20/30/40/50-tick minimum stops, and 0.25/0.50/0.75/1.00 ATR stops. Orders must actually fill within two hours. Outcomes are tested at 1R–5R under $500/$1,000/$1,500 nominal risk policies. Selection uses 2021–2024 only; 2025 remains report-only.
